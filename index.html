Who is Tarik

I'm a content creator from Morocco, now in Wrocław, where I'm studying for an MA in Communication Management at the University of Wrocław. I make short-form videos, AI-powered visuals and social media content that help brands connect with people.

Before content creation I spent years in front of audiences as a DJ, dancer and hotel entertainer, and I volunteered with AIESEC, organising events and working with students. That taught me how to read a room and how to make people feel welcome. My goal is simple: create content that feels human and makes people want to be part of the story.
